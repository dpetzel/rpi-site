# Pi 5 Components

!!! tip "Pi 5 components have moved to the interactive Boardview"
    All identified Pi 5 components, top and bottom, including their function, part numbers, replacement links
    and sources, are now on the **[Pi 5 Boardview](boardview/index.md)**.

    - Click a part to see its details.
    - Search by reference or value (e.g. `J1`, `DA9091`, `PMZB600`).
    - Use **Top / Bottom** (or press <kbd>F</kbd>) to switch sides.
    - Click a pad to highlight its net.
